What year one actually costs
$2,000 setup, once — or $1,000 up front on the split start. Then voice usage on your own account, at cost: roughly $60–$150 a month for a practice handling 300 answered calls. A care plan is optional at $149 or $299 a month.

Without a care plan that is about $250–$350 a month equivalent across year one, and year two costs only the usage. With the PMS plan it is nearer $460–$550. No markup on usage, and nothing else bills monthly.
